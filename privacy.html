Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the GeoTime application by Matthew F. Carroll, currently available as a web app, on iOS and on Android.

GeoTime collects nothing about you by default, and there is no account to create in order to use it. The app requests access to your device's location for the sole purpose of displaying the current time and timezone at your location. Your coordinates are used only while the app is running, and are never transmitted to our servers or to any third party. The app stores the name of the city or town closest to your last known position on your device, so the home-screen widget can label your local time while the app is closed. That name is never transmitted anywhere, including to the people you share your time with.

One optional feature does send something, and only if you turn it on. It is described in “Sharing your time” below.

GeoTime can also show “ship time” — the onboard clock on a Royal Caribbean or Celebrity cruise, which shifts during a sailing. Because that clock is set by the crew and cannot be worked out from a map, the app fetches the current ship time from Royal Caribbean when you add a ship to your world clock list, and while you are connected to a ship's wi-fi, to recognise which ship you are aboard. Those requests identify only the ship name — never your location, device or account identifiers, or personal information of any kind.

Sharing your time

GeoTime can show you what time it is for someone else — a partner in another country, a parent on a cruise — on your world clock list and your home-screen widget. This is entirely optional. Nothing described in this section happens, and no record of you exists on our server, unless and until you tap “Share your time” or enter somebody's code.

When you do, our server creates an account for you under a random identifier, and gives this device a random key of its own, which it keeps. The server stores only a scrambled form of that key, so its records cannot be used to act as you. None of it is tied to your email address, phone number, your device's own identifiers, or any account you hold elsewhere; there is no password, because there is nothing to sign in to.

It asks you for a name, and that name is sent — it is what the people you share with see above your clock, and without it their screen would show a time with nothing to say whose it is. Put whatever you like in it; nothing checks it against anything. Whoever you share with can rename you on their own device afterwards, and that renaming happens entirely on their phone: we never see it.

While sharing is on, the app sends the server what time it is where you are, and nothing more. In practice that is a timezone name such as America/Vancouver — or, aboard a ship, that ship's name and how many hours its clock is from UTC.

Nothing narrower than a timezone is ever sent. Not your coordinates, and not the name of your town either. A timezone is thousands of kilometres wide and shared by millions of people: everyone from Vancouver to Nelson to Los Angeles keeps the same one, and nothing in what we send distinguishes them.

By default they are not even told which timezone. Unless you turn on “Share my exact timezone”, the only thing our server passes on is how many hours your clock is from theirs — a number, with no region attached. Turning it on tells them the timezone by name, which is a region and never a place; the app's map deliberately lights up the whole band of that time rather than the single zone, and the Sharing screen shows you a preview of the row they will see so you can decide with it in front of you. Either way they see what time it is for you and not where you are.

No history is kept either. Each update replaces the last, so there is no record of where you have been or when. The only date kept with your time is when it was last updated, which is what lets the other person see that an answer has gone stale. Beyond that the server notes when your account and each linked device were created and last used, and when each share was made and accepted — dates, never places — which is what lets you tell your devices apart and lets an account nobody has opened in a long time eventually be removed.

The name you give somebody you follow — “Mum”, “Dad” — is stored only on your own device and is never sent anywhere. The server does not know it and cannot be asked for it.

You can use the same account on more than one device — a tablet, or a browser on your computer — by linking it from a device that already is. The device already linked shows a code that works once and stops working after 10 minutes, and it has to approve the new device before that device can see anything. For each device linked to your account, the server records what kind it is (“iPhone”, “Chrome on macOS”), whether it is the one that shares your time, and when it was last used, so that you can recognise each one and remove it under “Your devices”. Only one device shares your time, and a browser never does.

Codes are single-use and stop working after 24 hours. A share, once accepted, lasts until either of you ends it: removing somebody from your list also stops their time reaching you, and removing a follower stops your time reaching them. Either side can do this at any moment without the other's agreement.

The server is a small application run by us on Cloudflare's network, which processes the requests on our behalf. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build a profile, and there is no analytics or tracking in the app at all.

Deleting your data

In GeoTime, open the Sharing card and tap Delete my sharing data at the bottom, then confirm. That deletes your account, every device linked to it, your name, your current time, and every share you are part of, immediately and permanently. The people you follow stop reaching you, and the people who follow you stop seeing your time. Nothing is retained after deletion, and there is no backup copy to ask us for.

You can do this from any of your linked devices, including a browser linked at geotime.app. To remove just one device and keep the rest, tap Remove or Unlink beside it under “Your devices” instead.

If you no longer have any device with GeoTime on it, nothing we hold can be matched to you: there is no email address, phone number or password on the account, so we have no way to tell which one was yours. The people who followed you can remove you from their own lists at any time, which ends those shares. If you have never tapped “Share your time” or followed anybody, there is nothing on our server to delete.
