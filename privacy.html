Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the GeoTime application by Matthew F. Carroll, currently available as a web app, on iOS and on Android.

GeoTime collects nothing about you by default, and no account is needed to use it. It uses your device's location, only while it is running, to work out your timezone and nearby town on your device. Your location is never sent to our server.

For ships on your list, the app fetches the ship's time and position, and while you are on a ship's wi-fi it checks which ship you are aboard. Those requests name only the ship, never you, your location or your device.

The app uses maps drawn by Google Maps, so, as with any map, Google supplies the map of the area being shown, which is usually the area around you. We do not transmit your actual coordinates to Google Maps.

Sharing your time

GeoTime can show you what time it is for someone else — a partner in another country, a parent on a cruise — on your world clock list and your home-screen widget. This is entirely optional. Nothing described in this section happens, and no record of you exists on our server, unless and until you tap “Share your time” or enter somebody's code.

When you do, our server creates an account for you under a random identifier, and gives this device a random key of its own. The server stores only a scrambled form of that key, so its records cannot be used to act as you. None of it is tied to your email address, phone number, your device's own hardware identifiers, or any account you hold elsewhere.

The sharing feature asks you for a name, which is sent to our server. The people you share your time with see it above your clock, and the people you follow see it in their list of who can see their time. Whoever you share with can rename you on their own device. Those labels, like the names you give the people you follow, never leave the device they were set on.

While sharing is on, the app sends the server what time it is where you are, and nothing more. In practice that is a timezone name such as America/Vancouver — or, aboard a ship, that ship's name and how many hours its clock is from UTC.

The exact timezone label is only shared if you choose. By default, the only thing our server passes on is how many hours your clock is from theirs — a number, with no region attached. Turning on “Share my exact timezone” tells them the timezone by name, for example “Vancouver” or “Los Angeles”. The sharing screen shows you a preview of what they will see. Aboard a ship with it on, they see the ship's name, and a ship's position is public.

No history is kept. When your main device sends an update, the old one is removed, so there is no record of where you have been. We keep only dates — when your account, devices and shares were created and last used, never places — so you can tell your devices apart and so an account unused for a long time can be removed.

You can use the same account on more than one device — a tablet, or a browser on your computer — by linking it. Your existing device has to approve the new one before it can see anything. The time you share with others comes from your main device. For each device linked to your account, the server records what kind it is (“iPhone”, “Chrome on macOS”), whether it is your main device, and when it was last used, so that you can recognise each one and remove it under “Your devices”.

Sharing codes are single-use and stop working after 24 hours. A share can be removed at any time: removing somebody from your list stops their time reaching you, and removing a follower stops your time reaching them. Following someone does not let them see your time: if you both want to see each other's clocks, you share yours with them too.

The server is a small application run by us on Cloudflare's network, which processes the requests on our behalf. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build a profile, and there is no analytics or tracking in the app at all.

Deleting your data

In GeoTime, open the Sharing card and tap Delete my sharing data at the bottom, then confirm. That deletes your account, every device linked to it, your name, your current time, and every share you are part of, immediately and permanently. You will stop seeing the people you follow, and the people who follow you stop seeing your time. Nothing is retained after deletion, and there is no backup copy to ask us for.

You can do this from any of your linked devices, including a browser linked at geotime.app. To remove just one device and keep the rest, tap Remove or Unlink beside it under “Your devices” instead.

If you no longer have any device with GeoTime on it, nothing we hold can be matched to you: there is no email address, phone number or password on the account, so we have no way to tell which one was yours. If you have never tapped “Share your time” or followed anybody, there is nothing on our server to delete.
