Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the GeoTime application by Matthew F. Carroll, currently available as a web app, on iOS and on Android.

GeoTime collects nothing about you by default, and there is no account to create in order to use it. The app requests access to your device's location for the sole purpose of displaying the current time and timezone at your location. Your coordinates are used only while the app is running, and are never transmitted to our servers or to any third party. The app stores the name of the city or town closest to your last known position on your device, so the home-screen widget can label your local time while the app is closed.

One optional feature does send something, and only if you turn it on. It is described in “Sharing your time” below.

GeoTime can also show “ship time” — the onboard clock on a Royal Caribbean or Celebrity cruise, which shifts during a sailing. Because that clock is set by the crew and cannot be worked out from a map, the app fetches the current ship time from Royal Caribbean when you add a ship to your world clock list, and while you are connected to a ship's wi-fi, to recognise which ship you are aboard. Those requests identify only the ship name — never your location, device or account identifiers, or personal information of any kind.

Sharing your time

GeoTime can show you what time it is for someone else — a partner in another country, a parent on a cruise — on your world clock list and your home-screen widget. This is entirely optional. Nothing described in this section happens, and no record of you exists on our server, unless and until you tap “Share your time” or enter somebody's code.

When you do, the app asks our server for a random identifier and keeps it on your device. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, device, or any account you hold elsewhere; there is no password, because there is nothing to sign in to.

While sharing is on, the app sends the server what time it is where you are, and nothing more. In practice that is a timezone name such as America/Vancouver, together with the nearest town's name if your device has worked one out; or, aboard a ship, that ship's name and how many hours its clock is from UTC.

Your coordinates are never sent. There is nowhere in the record to put them, and the app does not ask the server to hold them. The most precise thing about you that ever leaves your device is the name of your nearest town, and only while sharing is on. That is deliberate: “Birmingham” is what makes the row worth reading, where a timezone name on its own is not. It is a town and not a street or a position. The app includes it whenever it has worked one out, and there is no separate switch for it — turning sharing off, or ending a share, is how you stop sending it.

No history is kept either way. Each update replaces the last, so there is no record of where you have been or when. The only date stored is when your current time was last updated, which is what lets the other person see that an answer has gone stale.

The name you give somebody you follow — “Mum”, “Dad” — is stored only on your own device and is never sent anywhere. The server does not know it and cannot be asked for it.

Codes are single-use and stop working after 24 hours. A share, once accepted, lasts until either of you ends it: removing somebody from your list also stops their time reaching you, and removing a follower stops your time reaching them. Either side can do this at any moment without the other's agreement.

You can delete everything at once from the Sharing card in the app. That removes your identifier, your current time, and every share you are part of, immediately and permanently, and every share you were part of stops working for the other person too. Nothing is retained after deletion, and there is no backup copy to ask us for.

The server is a small application run by us on Cloudflare's network, which processes the requests on our behalf. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build a profile, and there is no analytics or tracking in the app at all.
